02 / Who it's for

One handshake.
Three hands.

Brands, people and partners usually meet through a long chain of tools and vendors. We think they should meet in one place, with trust built in from the first step.

For brands

Know who you're reaching before you pay to reach them.

Relevant, verified audiences. Meaningful engagement. And a clear line from what you spent to what came back.

For people

Your community. Relevant opportunities. Trusted access.

Show who you are once, simply. Find what's open to your community. We explain what information is needed and why.

For partners

Bring what you do best to a much bigger room.

Connect your technology, reach or execution to a wider network of brands and verified audiences.

03 / What we won't compromise on

Trust isn't a feature.
It's the foundation.

i.Privacy by design

Only what's needed, used for the reason we gave you. Built into how it works, not added to the small print.

ii.A fair exchange

When you share something, it should be clear what you get in return. If it isn't clear, we haven't done our job.

iii.Proof over promises

No big adjectives. Just outcomes you can see and measure.
